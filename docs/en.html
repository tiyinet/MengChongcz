01What it is

A small Windows game that stitches a desktop pet together with a survivor-like. The core loop is only two things, but it holds up for dozens of hours.

Move with mouse or WASD

Attacks are automatic. Your whole job is deciding where to walk and when to stop pushing.

Return to your owner to recharge

Fill the meter and unleash a combo. Spend it early and you waste it; spend it late and you never get the chance.

Clear 5 stages, it moves in

Desktop pet mode unlocks the game window as a desktop companion. It wanders, yawns, and chases your cursor.

02Sixteen pets, four worlds

Six cats, four dogs, four birds, two turtles. Each has its own sprite and four evolution branches. The worlds run from a living room to a night forest.

Cats — black, orange, blue, ragdoll, tabby, white

Fast and evasive, built for kiting. The orange cat has wider range but moves slower.

Dogs — corgi, golden, German shepherd, border collie

Tanky and strong up close. The golden's combo is full-screen; the shepherd has the highest single-target burst.

Birds — cockatiel, budgie, peach-faced, pigeon

They fly over terrain and hit from range. The trade-off is low HP.

Turtles — red-eared, map turtle

Slow but sturdy. Highest defense growth, made for grinding it out.

Four worlds, 40 stages

Living room, balcony, garden, night forest — 10 stages plus a boss each. There is a hidden stage past the forest.

Twelve pest entries

Ants, flies, mosquitoes, roaches, spiders, geckos, centipedes, mice, and four world bosses.

05FAQ

What genre is this?

A desktop pet survivor game — survivor-like / bullet heaven. You raise a pet, take it into battle, dodge swarms across 40 stages. Clearing stage 5 unlocks a desktop pet mode where the pet lives on your Windows desktop.

Is it really free? Any ads or microtransactions?

Completely free. No microtransactions, no gacha, no ads, no account, no network connection required. Unzip and play.

Will it run on an older laptop?

Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit. The package is about 46 MB and needs no dedicated GPU — integrated graphics are fine.

Windows SmartScreen warns me when I run it. Is that a problem?

The installer has no code-signing certificate, so this warning is expected and does not mean the file is malicious. Click "More info" then "Run anyway". To verify, run certutil -hashfile "MengChongcz_Setup.zip" SHA256 and compare against the published hash.

Where are saves stored?

In a save folder inside the game directory, not the registry. Copy the folder and your progress moves with you.

Is there a mobile or Mac version?

Windows only for now.
